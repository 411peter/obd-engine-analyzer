Clear all engine codes?

      
This erases stored codes, freeze frame data and readiness monitors. Write the codes down first. If the fault is still there, the light will come back. Do this with the ignition on and the engine off.

      

    

  


  
  

    
Why is the light on?

    
For the most useful result: read codes, warm the engine fully, let it idle for 1 minute, then hold about 2,500 rpm for 30 seconds (in park/neutral).
